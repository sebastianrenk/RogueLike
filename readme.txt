# Terminal Rogue - like game

# Symbols
  @ = Player
  # = Wall
  . = empty space
  M = Monster
  X = Goal

# How to move:
  Press w to go up, a to go left, s to go down, d to go right

# You start with a total of 5 lives. 
  If you hit a monster you will lose 1 life. 

# There is a total of 10 levels. Reach every goal and beat the game!

# Enjoy your time and thank you for playing!
